# Family Game Night

A pass-the-tablet game portal for the family. The home screen lets you pick a game:

- **Spelling Bee** (`bee.html`): players sit in a circle. The next player reads each word, its meaning, and a sentence, and the speller spells it out loud. Run out of hearts and you're out, and the words get harder as players drop out. Everyone plays at their own level (Little Bees, Busy Bees, Honey Masters, Queen Bees).
- **Charade Parade** (`act.html`): the guesser holds the tablet on their forehead while everyone else acts out, describes or hums the word. Tilt down for got it, tilt up to pass, or tap the right or left side. Kid decks show a picture on every card so non-readers can act too.

Play it: https://austinberezniak32.github.io/family-spelling-bee/

Install on a tablet: open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to home screen** (or **Install app**). After the first open it works offline.

When you change files, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` so installed copies pick up the update.
